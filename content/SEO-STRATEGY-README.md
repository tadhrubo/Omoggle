## WHY THESE PIECES, IN THIS ORDER

### 1. "What Is Mogging" — Your Most Important Article

This targets the highest-volume query in your cultural niche. Anyone who hears the word "mogging" for the first time goes to Google. This article captures that traffic AND ends with a direct CTA to enter the arena. It also builds topical authority — Google needs to see you as the definitive source on mogging content before it ranks you for brand queries.

**Estimated monthly search volume:** 40,000–80,000 globally, growing.

### 2. "Omegle Alternatives 2026"

This is your competitor SEO play. "Omegle alternative" has enormous residual search volume from the 2023 shutdown and continues to generate searches from people looking for where to go. You rank #1 for this, you get the exact user who is looking for a platform like yours. 

**Estimated monthly search volume:** 100,000–300,000 globally.

### 3. PSL Rating — Existing Article Expanded

You already link to this from your homepage. Expanding it to full depth means it captures all the related queries: "canthal tilt explained", "jawline rating scale", "PSL scale meaning" etc. Each of these is a low-competition, high-intent query from your exact target demographic.

### 4. Looksmaxxing Guide

"Looksmaxxing" as a search term has exploded. This article is your bridge between the general looksmaxxing community and your specific platform. It ends with Omoggle's Solo Calibration as the measurement tool — turning informational intent into product engagement.

### 5–6. Hunter Eyes + Mog Battle Strategy

These target the more specific, lower-volume but higher-intent queries from users who are already deep in the community. Lower competition, easier to rank, and the audience is exactly who you want.

## INTERNAL LINKING STRUCTURE

Every blog post links back to the arena (`omoggle.games`).
Every blog post links to at least one other blog post.
The FAQ page links to relevant blog posts for extended answers.

This builds a content cluster that Google's Helpful Content algorithm rewards.

```
omoggle.games (homepage)
├── /blog/what-is-mogging → links to PSL rating, arena
├── /blog/omegle-alternatives-2026 → links to arena, what-is-mogging

```

## TECHNICAL SEO CHECKLIST (per article)

- [ ] Slug matches primary keyword exactly (`/blog/what-is-mogging`)
- [ ] H1 contains primary keyword
- [ ] First 100 words contain primary keyword naturally
- [ ] Internal links to 2+ other pages
- [ ] External links to 1–2 authoritative sources (Wikipedia, PubMed where relevant)
- [ ] Images with descriptive alt text
- [ ] Article word count 1,500–3,000 words (all articles above are in this range)
- [ ] FAQ section at bottom of each article (targets People Also Ask boxes)
- [ ] Canonical URL set in frontmatter

## FAQ PAGE IMPLEMENTATION NOTE

The FAQ page content is structured for JSON-LD FAQPage schema. Make sure it's also
added to your layout.tsx `jsonLd` object as the FAQPage schema — the file already
contains this in the `@graph` array. FAQPage schema is one of the fastest paths
to appearing in Google's People Also Ask boxes, which drive significant brand-query traffic.
