# Omoggle App

## Play Mog Battles on Any Device

Omoggle works on every device with a camera and a modern browser. No download required. No App Store approval wait. No storage used on your device.

Open your browser. Go to **omoggle.games**. Battle.

---

## Mobile Browser (Recommended — No Download)

The fastest way to play on mobile is directly through your browser:

**On iPhone (iOS):** Open Safari → go to omoggle.games → tap Allow when prompted for camera and microphone access → enter the arena.

**On Android:** Open Chrome → go to omoggle.games → tap Allow for camera and microphone → enter the arena.

The mobile browser experience is fully featured — all battle modes, ELO ranking, leaderboard access, and profile management work identically to desktop.

**Add to Home Screen for app-like experience:**

iOS Safari: tap the Share icon → "Add to Home Screen" → Omoggle appears as an icon on your home screen and opens in fullscreen without the browser chrome.

Android Chrome: tap the three-dot menu → "Add to Home Screen" → same result.

This gives you a native app feel without any download.

## System Requirements

**iOS:** Safari on iOS 14 or later. iPhone 8 and above recommended for consistent video performance.

**Android:** Chrome on Android 8.0 or later. Any device with a front-facing camera.

**Desktop:** Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge. Any operating system.

**Internet connection:** 5 Mbps upload/download minimum. 10+ Mbps recommended for best video quality. Wi-Fi or 5G preferred over 4G for ranked battles.

## Camera Tips for Mobile Battles

Playing on mobile introduces some specific setup considerations vs. desktop:

**Hold the phone at chin height or below** — most front cameras are at the top of the phone. Holding it low creates the upward angle that emphasizes jaw structure and brow bone. Holding it above eye level looking down flattens your face.

**Landscape vs. Portrait:** Both work. Portrait is the default for mobile users. Landscape gives the audience a wider view of your face with more context. Experiment with both.

**Lighting on mobile:** The same rules apply as desktop. Front-facing light, avoid backlighting, avoid overhead only. A window in front of you at angle works well for mobile battles.

**Stabilize the phone:** A shaky camera reads as nervous and distracting. Use a phone stand, prop it against something, or hold it with both hands braced against your body. Stillness reads as composure.

## Omoggle Games: All Available Modes

Whether on mobile or desktop, all battle modes are available:

**1v1 Mog Battle** — Random matchmaking against a player at your ELO level. 30-second live battle, audience votes, result updates your rank.

**Ranked Match** — ELO-strict matchmaking. Tighter tier matching, higher stakes ELO movement.

**Solo Calibration** — Face an audience alone. Get a base score without a direct opponent. Best for new players establishing a baseline or players testing a new camera setup.

**Private Room** — Generate a 6-character code, share with anyone, battle directly. Rematches unlimited. Optional ELO mode.
